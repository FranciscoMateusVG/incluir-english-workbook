Exercise 1 Some or Any?

Complete each sentence with some or any. (Dica: some em frases afirmativas; any em negativas e perguntas.)

1. I need new socks for winter. some
2. We don't have milk at home. any
3. Do you have questions about the homework? any
4. She bought beautiful dresses at the mall. some
5. There aren't shirts in my size. any
6. I have money for the bus. some
Exercise 2 Much, Many, A Few, or A Little?

Circle the correct quantifier. Remember: many / a few with countable nouns, much / a little with uncountable nouns.

1.

How _______ shoes do you have?

  • a) much
  • b) many ✓ correct
  • c) a little
2.

How _______ money do you need for the shopping trip?

  • a) much ✓ correct
  • b) many
  • c) a few
3.

I only have _______ minutes before my bus leaves.

  • a) a little
  • b) much
  • c) a few ✓ correct
4.

There is _______ sugar in this coffee. It is almost bitter.

  • a) a little ✓ correct
  • b) many
  • c) a few
5.

We don't have _______ time before the store closes.

  • a) many
  • b) much ✓ correct
  • c) a few
6.

_______ people were at the market this morning. It was very full!

  • a) Much
  • b) A little
  • c) Many ✓ correct

Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with a quantifier. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

I don't have some money for new shoes.

Correct: I don't have any money for new shoes.
2.

How many milk do we need for the cake?

Correct: How much milk do we need for the cake?
3.

There are much people in the store today.

Correct: There are many people in the store today.
4.

She only has a few sugar in her kitchen.

Correct: She only has a little sugar in her kitchen.
5.

Do you have some jackets in blue?

Correct: Do you have any jackets in blue?
