Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with there is / there are or a demonstrative. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

There is many restaurants in my neighbourhood.

Correct: There are many restaurants in my neighbourhood.
2.

This shoes are too small for me.

Correct: These shoes are too small for me.
3.

There are a hospital on the main avenue.

Correct: There is a hospital on the main avenue.
4.

Those book on that shelf over there is very old.

Correct: That book on that shelf over there is very old.
5.

These is my new apartment. Welcome!

Correct: This is my new apartment. Welcome!
