Exercise 4 Rewrite with the Connector

Rewrite the two sentences using the connector in parentheses. Remember the position: also before the verb, too at the end, However and Then at the beginning of a new sentence.

1.

I bought flour. I bought sugar. (also)

Correct: I bought flour. I also bought sugar.
2.

The film was long. It was interesting. (However)

Correct: The film was long. However, it was interesting.
3.

It was cold. We closed the windows. (so)

Correct: It was cold, so we closed the windows.
4.

My sister plays the guitar. My brother plays the guitar. (too)

Correct: My sister plays the guitar. My brother plays the guitar, too.
5.

Mix the eggs. Add the milk. (Then)

Correct: Mix the eggs. Then, add the milk.
