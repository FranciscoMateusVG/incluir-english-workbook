Exercise 2 Do or Does?

Circle the correct option to complete each question or negative sentence.

1.

_______ you like Brazilian music?

  • a) Do ✓ correct
  • b) Does
  • c) Is
2.

_______ your brother work on Saturdays?

  • a) Do
  • b) Does ✓ correct
  • c) Are
3.

She _______ eat meat. She is a vegetarian.

  • a) don't
  • b) doesn't ✓ correct
  • c) isn't
4.

Where _______ they live?

  • a) do ✓ correct
  • b) does
  • c) is
5.

What time _______ the class start?

  • a) do
  • b) does ✓ correct
  • c) are

Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with the Present Simple or the question word. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

My father drive a taxi in the city.

Correct: My father drives a taxi in the city.
2.

She don't work on Sundays.

Correct: She doesn't work on Sundays.
3.

Does they study English at night?

Correct: Do they study English at night?
4.

Where does you live?

Correct: Where do you live?
5.

Who is your teacher? — What is Ms. Clara.

Correct: Who is your teacher? — It is Ms. Clara. (The answer to "Who" is a person, not "What".)
6.

My brother watchs football every weekend.

Correct: My brother watches football every weekend.
