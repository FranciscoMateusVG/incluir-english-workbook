Exercise 3 Make Questions

Use the words to write a question in the Present Continuous. (Dica: comece com Am / Is / Are ou com a palavra de pergunta.)

1.

you / work / today?

Correct: Are you working today?
2.

she / cook / lunch?

Correct: Is she cooking lunch?
3.

the children / watch / TV?

Correct: Are the children watching TV?
4.

what / you / do / right now?

Correct: What are you doing right now?
5.

where / they / go?

Correct: Where are they going?
