Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with a plural or an uncountable noun. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

There are three childs in the park.

Correct: There are three children in the park.
2.

I bought two waters bottles at the bakery.

Correct: I bought two bottles of water at the bakery.
3.

Many persons work in this building.

Correct: Many people work in this building.
4.

The mouses are a problem in the old kitchen.

Correct: The mice are a problem in the old kitchen.
5.

We need two breads for the sandwiches.

Correct: We need two loaves of bread for the sandwiches. (Also accepted: some bread)
6.

My two brother-in-laws live in Salvador.

Correct: My two brothers-in-law live in Salvador.
