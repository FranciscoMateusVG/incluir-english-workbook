Exercise 3 Join the Sentences

Join the two sentences into one, using the connector in parentheses. Write the new sentence on the line.

1.

I was tired. I went to bed early. (so)

Correct: I was tired, so I went to bed early.
2.

She opened the window. It was hot. (because)

Correct: She opened the window because it was hot.
3.

He likes football. He doesn't like volleyball. (but)

Correct: He likes football, but he doesn't like volleyball.
4.

You can have tea. You can have coffee. (or)

Correct: You can have tea, or you can have coffee.
5.

I finished work. I went to the supermarket. (after)

Correct: After I finished work, I went to the supermarket. (ou: I went to the supermarket after I finished work.)
