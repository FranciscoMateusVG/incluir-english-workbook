Exercise 2 A, An, or The?

Circle the correct article to complete each sentence.

1.

_______ sun is very strong today.

  • a) A
  • b) An
  • c) The ✓ correct
2.

There is a bakery on my street. _______ bakery opens at six.

  • a) A
  • b) The ✓ correct
  • c) An
3.

I have _______ old bicycle.

  • a) a
  • b) an ✓ correct
  • c) the
4.

_______ Amazon River is in the north of Brazil.

  • a) The ✓ correct
  • b) A
  • c) An
5.

Which sentence is correct?

  • a) I love the dogs. They are friendly animals.

Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with an article. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

She is an teacher at my school.

Correct: She is a teacher at my school.
2.

I waited for a hour at the bus stop.

Correct: I waited for an hour at the bus stop.
3.

A moon is very bright tonight.

Correct: The moon is very bright tonight.
4.

My cousin wants to buy an house in Recife.

Correct: My cousin wants to buy a house in Recife.
5.

The coffee is my favorite drink in general.

Correct: Coffee is my favorite drink in general. (No article for general ideas.)
