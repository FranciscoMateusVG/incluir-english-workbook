Part III — Reading Comprehension (10 points)

Regina's Saturday Read the text and answer the questions below.

Regina lives in Belo Horizonte. From Monday to Friday, she works at a hospital, so Saturday is her favorite day. Every Saturday morning, she goes to the market near her house. She buys some fruit, a few vegetables, and a little coffee. She doesn't buy much meat because it is expensive.

Today is Saturday, and right now Regina is not at the market. She is cleaning her apartment because her sister is coming to visit. Her two children are helping her. Pedro is washing the dishes, and Luiza is putting the clothes in the closet.

In the afternoon, they always relax together. First, they have lunch. Then, they watch a movie. Finally, they walk in the park near the bakery. Regina loves Saturdays because the whole family is at home.
