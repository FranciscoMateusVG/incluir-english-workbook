Exercise 4 Describe and Explain

Answer with complete sentences.

1.

Describe a person in your family: one sentence with is, one with has.

Answers will vary. Model: My mother is short and friendly. She has long brown hair.
2.

How do you go to work, to school, or to the supermarket?

Answers will vary. Model: I go to work by bus. / I go to the supermarket on foot.
3.

Give simple directions from your house to the nearest bus stop (two steps).

Answers will vary. Model: Go straight on my street and turn right at the bakery. The bus stop is in front of the park.
