Exercise 2 Rewrite with the Adverb

Rewrite each sentence with the frequency adverb in brackets in the correct position.

1.

I check my phone in the morning. (always)

Correct: I always check my phone in the morning.
2.

My boss is happy on Fridays. (usually)

Correct: My boss is usually happy on Fridays.
3.

We eat fish. (rarely)

Correct: We rarely eat fish.
4.

She drinks tea at night. (never)

Correct: She never drinks tea at night.
5.

They visit their grandparents on Sundays. (often)

Correct: They often visit their grandparents on Sundays.
