Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with a plural or the Simple Present. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

She wake up at seven o'clock.

Correct: She wakes up at seven o'clock.
2.

I have three childs.

Correct: I have three children.
3.

He don't drink coffee at night.

Correct: He doesn't drink coffee at night.
4.

Does they study English on Mondays?

Correct: Do they study English on Mondays?
5.

There are two bus to the city center.

Correct: There are two buses to the city center.
