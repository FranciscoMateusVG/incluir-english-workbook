Verb To Be & Prepositions of Place

Exercise 1 Am, Is, or Are?

Complete each sentence with am, is, or are. (Dica: I → am; he / she / it → is; we / you / they → are.)

1. I a student at Incluir. am
2. My sister a nurse at the hospital. is
3. We from Brazil. are
4. The pens in my backpack. are
5. you tired today? Are
6. The bakery not open on Sundays. is
7. My parents at the supermarket now. are
8. It a beautiful day! is
Exercise 2 Where Is It? Choose the Correct Preposition

Circle the correct preposition of place to complete each sentence.

1.

The notebook is _______ the desk.

  • a)on ✓ correct
  • b)between
  • c)next
2.

The cat is _______ the bed. You can't see it.

  • a)on
  • b)under ✓ correct
  • c)in front of
3.

The pharmacy is _______ the bakery and the supermarket.

  • a)under
  • b)next
  • c)between ✓ correct
4.

My keys are _______ the kitchen.

  • a)in ✓ correct
  • b)at
  • c)behind
5.

  • a)under
  • b)in front of ✓ correct
  • c)on
Exercise 3 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with the verb to be. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

I is a teacher.

Correct: I am a teacher.
2.

They is my friends from work.

Correct: They are my friends from work.
3.

My brother are twenty years old.

Correct: My brother is twenty years old.
4.

You is a good student.

Correct: You are a good student.
5.

We am in the classroom.

Correct: We are in the classroom.
Exercise 4 Make It Negative and Make It a Question

Rewrite each sentence two times: first in the negative (is not / are not), then as a yes/no question.

1.

She is from Recife.

Negative: She is not (isn't) from Recife. — Question: Is she from Recife?
2.

They are at the bus stop.

Negative: They are not (aren't) at the bus stop. — Question: Are they at the bus stop?
3.

The library is open today.

Negative: The library is not (isn't) open today. — Question: Is the library open today?
