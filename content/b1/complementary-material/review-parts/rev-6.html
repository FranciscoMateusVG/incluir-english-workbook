Telling the Time & Time Prepositions

Exercise 1 Write the Time in Words

Write each time in words. (Dica: :15 = quarter past; :30 = half past; :45 = quarter to + próxima hora.)

1. 8:00 → eight o'clock
2. 9:30 → half past nine
3. 2:15 → quarter past two
4. 5:45 → quarter to six
5. 12:00 (day) → noon / midday
6. 12:00 (night) → midnight
7. 7:30 → half past seven
8. 10:45 → quarter to eleven
Exercise 2 At, In, or On?

Complete each sentence with at, in, or on. (Dica: at + hora; on + dia da semana / data; in + mês / ano / parte do dia.)

1. My English class starts seven o'clock. at
2. I have a doctor's appointment Tuesday. on
3. She always studies the morning. in
4. My vacation is July. in
5. The party is June 15th. on
6. I don't like to drive night. at
7. We have lunch together Sundays. on
8. He was born 1990. in
Exercise 3 Other Time Expressions

Circle the correct option: from…to, until, before, after, during, between…and.

1.

I work _______ Monday _______ Friday.

  • a)from / to ✓ correct
  • b)at / on
  • c)in / until
2.

I brush my teeth _______ breakfast, when I finish eating.

  • a)before
  • b)after ✓ correct
  • c)during
3.

Please don't use your phone _______ the class.

  • a)until
  • b)between
  • c)during ✓ correct
4.

The bakery is open _______ eight o'clock at night. Then it closes.

  • a)until ✓ correct
  • b)after
  • c)during
5.

My lunch break is _______ noon _______ one o'clock.

  • a)during / after
  • b)between / and ✓ correct
  • c)until / to
Exercise 4 Your Schedule

Answer each question about your week with a complete sentence. Use at, in, or on.

1.

What time do you wake up during the week?

Answers will vary. Model: I wake up at six o'clock in the morning.
2.

What day is your English class?

Answers will vary. Model: My English class is on Tuesday at seven o'clock.
3.

When is your birthday? (month)

Answers will vary. Model: My birthday is in October.
