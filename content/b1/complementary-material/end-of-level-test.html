Part III — Reading Comprehension (10 points)

Ana's Week Read the text and answer the questions below.

Ana is from Fortaleza, but now she lives in São Paulo with her husband and two children. She is a nurse at a big hospital. From Monday to Friday, she wakes up at half past five and goes to work by subway. Her house is next to a bakery, so she always buys fresh bread in the morning.

At home, everybody helps with the chores. Ana cooks dinner, her husband washes the dishes, and the children make their beds and set the table. On Saturdays, they do the laundry and clean the house together.

Ana studies English on Tuesday nights, at seven o'clock. Her school is not far: she goes on foot. Her teacher is tall, has short black hair, and is very friendly. Ana never misses a class because she loves to learn.
