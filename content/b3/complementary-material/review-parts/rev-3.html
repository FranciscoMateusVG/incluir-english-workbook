Exercise 1 Rights, Duties or Rules?

Complete each sentence with can, must, should, mustn't or can't. The meaning in brackets helps you. (Dica: can = direito; must = dever; mustn't / can't = proibição.)

1. Every citizen use the public library for free. (direito) can
2. You vote on election day. It is your duty. (dever) must
3. You throw trash in the river. It is against the law. (proibição) mustn't
4. Children study at a public school near their home. (direito) can
5. Neighbors keep the shared spaces clean. (recomendação) should
6. You park in a space for people with disabilities without a permit. (proibição) can't
7. Everyone pay for their bus ticket. (dever) must
8. Patients ask for a second opinion at the health center. (direito) can

Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

Everyone must respects the traffic rules.

Correct: Everyone must respect the traffic rules.
2.

You can to ask for help at the information desk.

Correct: You can ask for help at the information desk.
3.

She cans borrow three books from the library.

Correct: She can borrow three books from the library.
4.

We not should waste water during the dry season.

Correct: We shouldn't waste water during the dry season.
5.

You mustn't to feed the animals at the city park.

Correct: You mustn't feed the animals at the city park.
