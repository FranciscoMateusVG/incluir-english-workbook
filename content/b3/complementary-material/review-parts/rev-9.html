Exercise 1 Too or Enough?

Complete each sentence with too or enough and the word in parentheses, in the correct order. (Dica: too + adjetivo; adjetivo + enough; enough + substantivo.)

1. This restaurant is for a quiet dinner. (noisy) too noisy
2. The water at the beach is for swimming. (warm) warm enough
3. We don't have for everyone. (chairs) enough chairs
4. That tour is — it costs $200! (expensive) too expensive
5. Is your soup , or do you want me to heat it again? (hot) hot enough

7. The museum is to visit in one day — it's huge! (big) too big
8. He isn't to drive; he's only sixteen. (old) old enough

Exercise 4 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with an intensifier, too or enough. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

The beach was very perfect for our picnic.

Correct: The beach was absolutely perfect for our picnic.
2.

The room is enough big for the whole class.

Correct: The room is big enough for the whole class.
3.

It was too much expensive for us.

Correct: It was too expensive for us.
4.

I'm very exhausted after work.

Correct: I'm absolutely exhausted after work. (também aceito: completely exhausted)
5.

We don't have time enough to visit the museum.

Correct: We don't have enough time to visit the museum.
6.

The city center is absolutely busy on Fridays.

Correct: The city center is very busy on Fridays. (também aceito: really busy)
