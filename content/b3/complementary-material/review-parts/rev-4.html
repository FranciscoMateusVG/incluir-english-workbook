Exercise 3 Rewrite: Negatives and Questions

Rewrite each sentence following the instruction in brackets.

1.

She will arrive early. (negativa)

Correct: She won't (will not) arrive early.
2.

They are going to rent a car. (pergunta)

Correct: Are they going to rent a car?
3.

You will need a jacket at night. (pergunta)

Correct: Will you need a jacket at night?
4.

He is going to buy souvenirs. (negativa)

Correct: He isn't (is not) going to buy souvenirs.
5.

We will visit the museum on Saturday. (negativa)

Correct: We won't (will not) visit the museum on Saturday.
