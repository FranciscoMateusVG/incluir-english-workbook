Comparatives & Superlatives

Exercise 1 Smart Shopping: Compare the Options

Complete each sentence with the comparative or superlative form of the adjective in parentheses. (Dica: adjetivos curtos → -er / the -est; adjetivos longos → more / the most.)

1. This supermarket is than the one near my house. (cheap) cheaper
2. Fresh bread is than frozen bread. (good) better
3. That is phone in the shop. (expensive) the most expensive
4. Saturday is day at the street market. (busy) the busiest
5. This bag is than that one. (heavy) heavier
6. The service here is than before. (bad) worse
7. This is chair in the store. (comfortable) the most comfortable
8. My new blender is than my old one. (quiet) quieter

Exercise 3 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with a comparative or superlative. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

This store is more cheaper than the other one.

Correct: This store is cheaper than the other one.
2.

This is the most big supermarket in town.

Correct: This is the biggest supermarket in town.
3.

This brand is more better than that one.

Correct: This brand is better than that one.
4.

She is the taller person in our family.

Correct: She is the tallest person in our family.
5.

These shoes are as cheaper as the black ones.

Correct: These shoes are as cheap as the black ones.
6.

Today the market is busyer than yesterday.

Correct: Today the market is busier than yesterday.
Exercise 4 Rewrite with (Not) As ... As

Rewrite each idea using as ... as or not as ... as with the adjective in parentheses. Keep the same meaning.

1.

The small shop is quieter than the mall. (quiet) — Start with: The mall...

Correct: The mall is not as quiet as the small shop.
2.

The green bag costs $20. The blue bag also costs $20. (cheap)

Correct: The green bag is as cheap as the blue bag.
3.

The street market is smaller than the supermarket. (big) — Start with: The street market...

Correct: The street market is not as big as the supermarket.
4.

The chocolate cake and the orange cake are equally delicious. (delicious)

Correct: The chocolate cake is as delicious as the orange cake.
5.

The bus is slower than the train. (fast) — Start with: The bus...

Correct: The bus is not as fast as the train.
