Modal Verbs: Advice, Obligation & Prohibition

Exercise 1 Complete the Work Rules

Each sentence ends with a meaning in brackets. Choose the modal from the box that matches that meaning and write it in the blank. Use each option only once. (Dica: should = conselho; must / have to = obrigação; mustn't / can't = proibição.)

Word Bank
should shouldn't must have to has to don't have to mustn't can't
1. All workers wear a uniform. It is a company rule. (obrigação) must
2. You arrive ten minutes early on your first day. It makes a good impression. (conselho) should
3. We sign our names when we enter the building. (obrigação — nós) have to
4. Maria answer the phone at the front desk. It is her job. (obrigação — ela) has to
5. You smoke inside the kitchen. It is very dangerous. (proibição) mustn't
6. Visitors enter the storage room without a manager. (proibição) can't
7. You work on Sundays. The shop is closed. (não é necessário) don't have to
8. You eat lunch at your desk. The break room is more comfortable. (conselho negativo) shouldn't
Exercise 2 Choose the Correct Option

Circle the correct option to complete each sentence.

1.

You look tired. You _______ go to bed earlier.

  • a) should ✓ correct
  • b) mustn't
  • c) can't
2.

Drivers _______ stop at a red light. It is the law.

  • a) shouldn't
  • b) must ✓ correct
  • c) don't have to
3.

She _______ wear a suit. The office has a relaxed dress code.

  • a) mustn't
  • b) doesn't have to ✓ correct
  • c) has to
4.

You _______ use your phone during the safety training. It is not allowed.

  • a) mustn't ✓ correct
  • b) should
  • c) have to
5.

_______ I have to bring my own lunch, or is there a cafeteria?

  • a) Do ✓ correct
  • b) Does
  • c) Must
Exercise 3 Find and Correct the Mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake with a modal verb. Rewrite the sentence correctly on the line.

1.

You must to wear safety glasses in this area.

Correct: You must wear safety glasses in this area.
2.

She have to start work at eight o'clock.

Correct: She has to start work at eight o'clock.
3.

He shoulds ask the manager for help.

Correct: He should ask the manager for help.
4.

You don't must park in front of the entrance.

Correct: You mustn't park in front of the entrance.
5.

Do we should bring our documents tomorrow?

Correct: Should we bring our documents tomorrow?
Exercise 4 Give Advice with Should / Shouldn't

Read each situation. Write one piece of advice using should or shouldn't.

1.

My new colleague is always late for work.

Model: He should leave home earlier. / He shouldn't go to bed so late.
2.

I don't understand my new work schedule.

Model: You should ask your manager to explain it.
3.

Ana feels very nervous before her first day at the new job.

Model: She should prepare her clothes the night before. / She shouldn't worry so much.
4.

My phone battery always dies before lunch.

Model: You should charge it every night. / You shouldn't use it so much in the morning.
5.

Pedro wants to make friends at his new workplace.

Model: He should have lunch with his colleagues. / He should say hello to everyone in the morning.
