Instruções:

Tempo sugerido: 75 minutos. O teste tem quatro seções, da mais fácil para a mais difícil. Cada seção tem gramática e um pequeno texto para ler. Responda até onde conseguir. Quando as questões ficarem difíceis demais, pare — isso é esperado e não é um problema. (Suggested time: 75 minutes. The test has four sections, from easiest to hardest. Each section has grammar and a short reading text. Answer as far as you can. When the questions get too hard, stop — that is expected and it is not a problem.)

Administration:

When: before the student's first class, at intake. Time: up to 75 minutes — but the stop rule means most students use much less: a true beginner works only Section A (about 20 minutes). Read the instruction callout aloud in Portuguese. Reassure the student that stopping early is normal — most students do not finish all four sections. Do not explain grammar or vocabulary during the test. Procedural help in Portuguese is fine.

Scoring: every item is worth 1 point, right or wrong — no partial credit. Multi-blank items count as one point and require all blanks correct (accept the tolerant variants shown in the answers). Score each section out of 25, write the totals in the score table, then apply the Placement Rubric at the end of this teacher edition, top-down, first matching row wins. Section gate: 18 of 25. Absolute-beginner floor: below 10 on Section A.

Placement Rubric:

Score each section out of 25, then apply these rows top-down — the first matching row wins:

  • Section A below 10 → B0 (absolute beginner)
  • Section A below 18 → B1
  • A ≥ 18 and Section B below 18 → B2
  • A ≥ 18, B ≥ 18 and Section C below 18 → B3
  • A ≥ 18, B ≥ 18, C ≥ 18 and Section D below 18 → B4
  • All four sections ≥ 18 → beyond B4 — talk with the student about goals; the program capstone is B4.

Write the result in the Nível / Placement row of the score table on page 1, and tell the student their level positively: the level is where they will grow fastest, not a grade.

Borderline Conversation Check:

If the deciding section scored exactly 17 (one point below the gate), run a 5-minute conversation before placing. Use the prompt pair for that boundary and judge comfort, not perfection:

  • Section A = 17 (B1 or B2): easier — "Tell me about yourself: your name, your city, your work." (B1: to be + Simple Present) · harder — "What are you doing these days? What food is there in your kitchen?" (B2: Present Continuous, there is/are, quantifiers)
  • Section B = 17 (B2 or B3): easier — "Describe your neighborhood: what is there near your house?" (B2) · harder — "What did you do last weekend? What are your plans for next month?" (B3: past narration + future)
  • Section C = 17 (B3 or B4): easier — "Tell me about a trip you took." (B3: Simple Past) · harder — "Have you ever lost something important? What happened?" (B4: Present Perfect for experiences and problems)
  • Section D = 17 (B4 or beyond): the "beyond B4" rubric row already requires a goals conversation — use it; place B4 unless the student is clearly beyond the program.
  • Repair prompt (any boundary): say one sentence quickly and see if the student uses repair language ("Can you repeat, please?").

Comfortable on the harder prompt → place UP. Hesitant or silent → place DOWN. Default when still in doubt: place DOWN — a student who finds the first weeks easy gains confidence; a student who drowns in week one is a dropout risk.
