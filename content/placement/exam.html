Instruções:

Tempo sugerido: 40 minutos. O teste tem quatro seções, da mais fácil para a mais difícil. Responda até onde conseguir. Quando as questões ficarem difíceis demais, pare — isso é esperado e não é um problema. (Suggested time: 40 minutes. The test has four sections, from easiest to hardest. Answer as far as you can. When the questions get too hard, stop — that is expected and it is not a problem.)

Administration:

When: before the student's first class, at intake. Time: about 40 minutes (about 10 per section). Read the instruction callout aloud in Portuguese. Reassure the student that stopping early is normal — most students do not finish all four sections. Do not explain grammar or vocabulary during the test. Procedural help in Portuguese is fine.

Scoring: every item is worth 1 point, right or wrong — no partial credit. Multi-blank items count as one point and require all blanks correct (accept the tolerant variants shown in the answers). Score each section, write the totals in the score table, then apply the Placement Rubric at the end of this teacher edition, top-down, first matching row wins.

Placement Rubric:

Score each section, then apply these rows top-down — the first matching row wins:

  • Section A below 4 → B0 (absolute beginner)
  • Section A below 7 → B1
  • A ≥ 7 and Section B below 7 → B2
  • A ≥ 7, B ≥ 7 and Section C below 7 → B3
  • A ≥ 7, B ≥ 7, C ≥ 7 and Section D below 7 → B4
  • All four sections ≥ 7 → beyond B4 — talk with the student about goals; the program capstone is B4.

Write the result in the Placement / Nível row of the score table on page 1, and tell the student their level positively: the level is where they will grow fastest, not a grade.

Borderline Conversation Check:

If the deciding section scored exactly 6, run a 5-minute conversation before placing. Ask three graded prompts and judge comfort, not perfection:

  • Easier level prompt: "Tell me about your daily routine." (expects Simple Present fluency)
  • Target level prompt: "What did you do last weekend?" (expects past narration — B3 gate) or "Have you ever traveled by plane?" (expects Present Perfect — B4 gate)
  • Repair prompt: say one sentence quickly and see if the student uses repair language ("Can you repeat, please?").

Comfortable on the target-level prompt → place UP. Hesitant or silent → place DOWN. Default when still in doubt: place DOWN — a student who finds the first weeks easy gains confidence; a student who drowns in week one is a dropout risk.
